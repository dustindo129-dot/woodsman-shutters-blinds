Why choose our blinds

Blinds are a window covering made of horizontal or vertical slats that can be tilted, raised, or lowered to control the amount of light, privacy, and ventilation in a room. They come in a wide range of materials — wood, faux wood, metal, and fabric — in many styles, colors, and sizes to improve both the energy efficiency and appearance of your home.

We help you choose between an inside mount for a neat, built-in look or an outside mount for maximum coverage and light control, then measure each window precisely so everything fits and operates perfectly.

  • Horizontal & vertical slat styles
  • Wood, faux wood, metal & fabric options
  • Inside or outside mount
  • Adjustable light & privacy control
  • Improved energy efficiency
  • Custom-measured for a precise fit
