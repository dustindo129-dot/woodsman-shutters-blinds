Our Factory

A look inside the Garland, TX workshop where every WoodsMan product is designed, built, and finished by hand.

Made in Garland, TX

Built from the ground up

At WoodsMan Shutters and Blinds, we don't outsource — every shutter, blind, and shade is manufactured in our own Garland, TX facility. From the initial design to the final finish, our team handles each step on-site with precision tools and decades of combined experience.

By keeping production local, we maintain strict quality control, minimize lead times, and eliminate the markups that come with overseas manufacturing. The result? A better product delivered faster at an honest price — built right here in the DFW Metroplex.

  • In-house design, cutting & assembly
  • Precision machinery & hand finishing
  • Strict quality inspection on every piece
  • Faster turnaround — no overseas delays
  • Custom sizes, shapes & finishes
  • Direct savings passed on to you
