FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free consultations and quotes?

Yes. Every project starts with a free, no-obligation consultation. We discuss your goals, take precise measurements, and give you an honest quote.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Garland, TX and serve Dallas, Plano, Richardson, Mesquite, Rowlett and the greater DFW Metroplex.

What's the difference between shutters, blinds, and shades?

Shutters are solid, framed panels with adjustable louvers; blinds use tiltable slats; shades are fabric that rolls or folds. We'll help you choose the right fit for each room.

Can my window treatments be motorized?

Absolutely. Most of our shades and blinds can be motorized for app, remote, and voice control, with scheduling and smart-home automation.

Do you manufacture and install everything yourselves?

Yes. We fabricate in our own Garland factory and handle professional installation, so your treatments fit perfectly.

How long will my order take?

Because we build locally, our lead times are among the fastest in DFW. We'll give you a clear timeline at your consultation.
