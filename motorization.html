Motorization

Effortless app, remote, and voice control for your shades and blinds — comfort, convenience, and energy savings.

Smart & effortless

Why add motorization

Motorization brings smart-home convenience to your windows. Raise, lower, and tilt your shades and blinds from a remote, an app, or a simple voice command — no cords to tug and nothing to reach behind furniture for. It's ideal for hard-to-reach windows, large spans, and anyone who wants a cleaner, safer look.

Set schedules so your windows open with the morning and close at sunset to protect floors and furnishings, cut glare, and help regulate temperature. Whisper-quiet motors and rechargeable or hardwired power options fit any room and any style.

  • App, remote & voice control (Alexa / Google)
  • Scheduling & automation
  • Cordless — child & pet safe
  • Quiet, smooth motors
  • Rechargeable battery or hardwired
  • Works with most shades & blinds
