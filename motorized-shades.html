Why choose motorized shades

Motorized shades bring smart-home convenience to your windows. Raise, lower, and tilt them from a remote, an app on your phone, or a simple voice command — no cords to tug and nothing to reach behind furniture for. They're ideal for hard-to-reach windows, large spans, and anyone who wants a cleaner, safer look.

Set schedules so shades open with the morning and close at sunset to protect your floors and furnishings, cut glare, and help regulate indoor temperature. Whisper-quiet motors and a wide range of fabrics and opacities mean they fit any room and any style.

  • App, remote & voice control (Alexa / Google)
  • Scheduling & automation
  • Cordless — child & pet safe
  • Quiet, smooth motors
  • Rechargeable battery or hardwired options
  • Custom fabrics, colors & opacities
