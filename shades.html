Window Shades

Soft, light-filtering shades — roller, Roman, cellular, and zebra — custom-made to bring warmth and comfort to your windows.

Softness & light

Why choose our shades

Shades bring softness and warmth to a room while giving you precise control over light and privacy. Made from fabric that rolls, folds, or stacks, they come in roller, Roman, cellular (honeycomb), and zebra styles — each with its own look and function to suit any space.

Choose light-filtering fabrics that glow with soft daylight, or room-darkening and blackout options for bedrooms and media rooms. Energy-efficient cellular shades even trap air to help regulate indoor temperature and lower your bills — and any shade can be upgraded with motorization.

  • Roller, Roman, cellular & zebra styles
  • Light-filtering, room-darkening & blackout fabrics
  • Energy-efficient honeycomb options
  • Cordless & child-safe lifts
  • Motorization-ready
  • Custom-fit to each window
